The Hierophant tarot card meaning

Major Arcana V. The Hierophant represents shared wisdom: teachers, traditions and the structures we learn inside. It can point to a mentor, a course or a community that helps you grow.

Upright keywords

traditionlearningguidancebelonging

Reversed keywords

rebellionquestioning the rulesgoing your own way
Yes or noYes, if you follow the proper steps
LoveTraditional commitment and shared values, or a need to define the relationship on your own terms.
Work & moneyTraining, qualifications and working within an established system. Seek advice from someone experienced.

The Hierophant reversed

Reversed, you may be outgrowing old rules and looking for your own way of doing things. Question traditions that no longer fit.

The Wispling take: Tradition · Learning · Wisdom shared

The wise old owl has read every spellbook in the crypt library and would love to teach you. Please raise your wing before speaking. This card honours the teachers, rituals and lessons that shape us.

Journal prompt: Who taught you something you still use every day?
