The Empress tarot card meaning

Major Arcana III. The Empress is the card of growing and making. She rules over comfort, creativity and care, for others and for yourself. Whatever you tend now is likely to flourish.

Upright keywords

abundancenurturecreativitycomfort

Reversed keywords

neglectcreative blockovergiving
Yes or noYes
LoveWarmth, affection and a nurturing relationship. For some, a sign of family growth.
Work & moneyCreative work thrives. A good time for projects you care about and steady, organic growth.

The Empress reversed

Reversed, you may be giving so much to others that your own garden is empty, or feeling blocked creatively. Rest, nourish yourself and let ideas grow back.

The Wispling take: Abundance · Nurture · Creativity

Queen of the pumpkin patch, she makes everything around her grow: cozy blankets, warm soup, a garden that simply won't quit. The Empress is the card of making things and taking care of them.

Journal prompt: What are you growing in your life right now?
