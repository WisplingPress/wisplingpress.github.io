The Lovers tarot card meaning

Major Arcana VI. The Lovers is about connection and choice. Beyond romance, it asks you to choose what truly aligns with your values, and to commit to it wholeheartedly.

Upright keywords

loveharmonychoicesalignment

Reversed keywords

imbalanceindecisionmisaligned values
Yes or noYes
LoveA meaningful bond, a deepening relationship, or an important choice about love.
Work & moneyPartnerships and collaborations go well. Make decisions that match what you care about.

The Lovers reversed

Reversed, something is out of balance: a relationship, or a choice you are making against your own values. Honest conversations help.

The Wispling take: Connection · Choices · Harmony

Two ghosts, one heart and a matchmaking bat in the clouds. Some of life's biggest choices are really about who you want floating beside you.
