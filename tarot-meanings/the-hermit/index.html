The Hermit tarot card meaning

Major Arcana IX. The Hermit steps away from the noise with a lantern to look within. This is a time for reflection, study and finding your own answers.

Upright keywords

reflectionsolitudeinner guidancesearching

Reversed keywords

isolationlonelinessavoiding reflection
Yes or noMaybe: take time to reflect
LoveTime to understand yourself, or space within a relationship.
Work & moneyDeep, focused work. Take time to rethink your direction before your next move.

The Hermit reversed

Reversed, healthy solitude may have tipped into isolation, or you are too busy to think. Reconnect, or make space to reflect, whichever you have been missing.

The Wispling take: Solitude · Reflection · Inner light

The mummy has slipped away from the party for a quiet walk up the mountain. His lantern only lights one step at a time, and that is all you ever need.

Journal prompt: What does your perfect quiet night look like?
