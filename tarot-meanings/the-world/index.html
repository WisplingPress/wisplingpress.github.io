The World tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XXI. The World is the end of the journey that began with The Fool: completion, achievement and a sense of wholeness. Celebrate how far you have come.

Upright keywords

completionachievementwholenesscelebration

Reversed keywords

unfinished businessdelaysseeking closure
Yes or noYes
LoveFulfilment, commitment and a relationship that feels complete.
Work & moneyFinishing a big project, reaching a goal or expanding further afield.

The World reversed

Reversed, something is almost finished but not quite. Tie up the loose ends before moving on.

The Wispling take: Completion · Wholeness · Celebration

The ghost dances inside a wreath of autumn leaves while friends watch from every corner. You made it through the whole Major Arcana. Take a bow.
