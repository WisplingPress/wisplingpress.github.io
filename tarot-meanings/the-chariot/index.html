The Chariot tarot card meaning

Major Arcana VII. The Chariot is focused forward movement. Two forces pull in different directions and you hold the reins. With discipline and a clear goal, you can win.

Upright keywords

determinationmomentumwillpowervictory

Reversed keywords

lack of directionstalled progresspulling in two ways
Yes or noYes
LoveMoving a relationship forward, or taking charge of what you want.
Work & moneyAmbition pays off. A push towards a goal, a move or a competitive win.

The Chariot reversed

Reversed, you may be pulled in two directions or losing momentum. Decide where you are actually going before pushing harder.

The Wispling take: Determination · Momentum · Victory

Pulled by two very determined cats (who will run in opposite directions unless you steer), the pumpkin carriage is going places. Focus, hold the reins and roll on.

Journal prompt: What goal are you steering towards?
