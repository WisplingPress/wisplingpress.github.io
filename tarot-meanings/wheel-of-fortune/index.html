Wheel of Fortune tarot card meaning

Major Arcana X. The Wheel is always turning. This card marks a turning point, often a lucky one, and reminds you that nothing stays the same forever, which is good news whichever way things are going.

Upright keywords

changecyclesluckturning points

Reversed keywords

bad luckresistance to changerepeating patterns
Yes or noYes
LoveA fated meeting, a new phase or an unexpected turn.
Work & moneyOpportunities arrive suddenly. Be ready to act when luck turns your way.

Wheel of Fortune reversed

Reversed, a cycle may be repeating or you are resisting a change that is coming anyway. Focus on what you can control.

The Wispling take: Cycles · Luck · Change

Round and round go the phases of the moon. The cat on top knows the secret: what goes up comes down, and what comes down gets its turn at the top again.
