The Devil tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XV. The Devil shows the chains we put on ourselves: habits, attachments and fears. Look closely and the chains are often loose enough to slip off.

Upright keywords

temptationhabitsattachmentmaterialism

Reversed keywords

releasebreaking freereclaiming power
Yes or noNo
LoveIntense attraction or an unhealthy pattern worth examining.
Work & moneyWatch overspending and burnout. Question the golden handcuffs.

The Devil reversed

Reversed, you are breaking free from something that has held you back. Keep going.

The Wispling take: Temptation · Habits · Playful indulgence

The imp is offering unlimited candy. The chains are made of candy too, and look how loose they are. You can wander off any time you like.

Journal prompt: Which sweet habit is worth a second look?
