Judgement tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XX. Judgement is a wake-up call. It asks you to reflect honestly on where you have been and rise into the next version of yourself.

Judgement reversed

Reversed, you may be judging yourself too harshly or ignoring an inner calling. Be kind and listen.
