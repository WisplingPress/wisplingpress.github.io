The Sun tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XIX. The Sun is one of the happiest cards in the deck: joy, warmth, success and confidence. Things are bright, clear and going your way.

Upright keywords

joysuccesspositivityvitality

Reversed keywords

temporary cloudslow energyoverconfidence
Yes or noYes
LoveHappiness, warmth and a relationship full of fun.
Work & moneySuccess, recognition and good energy. Share your work with confidence.

The Sun reversed

Reversed, the sun is still there, just behind a cloud. Joy may be delayed or you may need to reconnect with what makes you happy.

The Wispling take: Joy · Success · Warmth

Even skeletons need their vitamin D. A pumpkin pony, a wall of smiling sunflowers and a very cheerful sun: this is the happiest card in the deck.
