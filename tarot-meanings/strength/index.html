Strength tarot card meaning

Major Arcana VIII. Strength is quiet courage. It is not force but patience and kindness, including kindness towards your own wild feelings. Gentle persistence wins here.

Upright keywords

couragepatiencecompassioninner strength

Reversed keywords

self-doubtfrustrationlow energy
Yes or noYes
LoveCompassion, patience and a steady, supportive bond.
Work & moneyKeep going calmly. Soft skills, resilience and persistence will carry you through.

Strength reversed

Reversed, self-doubt or frustration may be taking over. Be patient with yourself and rebuild your confidence in small steps.

The Wispling take: Gentle courage · Patience · Compassion

Real strength isn't roaring. It's a tiny ghost calmly scratching a giant sleepy cat behind the ears. Softness, it turns out, is a superpower.

Journal prompt: When has being gentle taken real courage?
