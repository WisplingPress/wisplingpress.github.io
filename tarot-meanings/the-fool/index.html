The Fool tarot card meaning

Major Arcana 0. The Fool is the first step of a journey. It asks you to trust the path before you can see all of it, to say yes to something new and to travel light. There is innocence here, and a willingness to learn by doing.

beginningsfreedomspontaneitytrust

Reversed keywords

hesitationrecklessnessholding back
Yes or noYes
LoveA fresh start, a playful new connection, or a relationship that needs more fun and less worry.
Work & moneyA new job, project or idea. Good for starting; check the details before you commit money.

The Fool reversed

Reversed, The Fool warns of leaping without looking, or the opposite: being so afraid of getting it wrong that you never start. Ask whether you need more planning or simply more nerve.

The Wispling take: New beginnings · Leaps of faith · Spontaneity

Our little ghost has packed a bindle full of snacks and is floating straight off the edge of the known map, with a very excited bat for company. Scary? A bit. Exciting? Absolutely. The Fool reminds you that every great adventure starts with one slightly silly step.

Journal prompt: What adventure would you float off a cliff for?
