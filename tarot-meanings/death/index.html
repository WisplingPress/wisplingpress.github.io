Death tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XIII. Death rarely means death. It is the card of endings that make room for beginnings: a chapter closing, a transformation, clearing the old to make space for the new.

Upright keywords

endingstransformationtransitionmaking space

Reversed keywords

resisting changestagnationfear of endings
Yes or noNo, but something better follows
LoveThe end of a phase, or a relationship transforming into something new.
Work & moneyA job, project or way of working ends to make way for something better.

Death reversed

Reversed, you may be clinging to something that has already ended. Letting go gently opens the next door.

The Wispling take: Endings · Transformation · Renewal

Don't panic, this reaper is a gardener. Something ends, something new sprouts, and the sun rises anyway. Death is the card of clearing space for what comes next.
