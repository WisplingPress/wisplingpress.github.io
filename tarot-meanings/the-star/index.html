The Star tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XVII. After the storm comes The Star. It is hope, healing and quiet inspiration, a sign that things are getting better and you are on the right path.

Upright keywords

hopehealingrenewalinspiration

Reversed keywords

discouragementlost faithdisconnection
Yes or noYes
LoveHealing, hope and a gentle, honest connection.
Work & moneyInspiration and renewed motivation. A good time for creative and long-term goals.

The Star reversed

Reversed, you may be feeling discouraged or disconnected from what inspires you. Small acts of hope bring the light back.

The Wispling take: Hope · Healing · Inspiration

After the storm, a quiet ghost pours starlight into the pond while a raven keeps watch. Breathe out. Everything is going to be okay.
