The High Priestess tarot card meaning

Major Arcana II. The High Priestess sits between the seen and the unseen. She asks you to slow down and listen to your intuition. Not everything needs to be solved today; some answers arrive in quiet moments.

Upright keywords

intuitionmysteryinner knowingstillness

Reversed keywords

ignored instinctssecretsconfusion
Yes or noMaybe: wait for more information
LoveUnspoken feelings, a slow-burn connection, or a need to trust what you sense rather than what you are told.
Work & moneyResearch before you act. Keep plans private for now and trust your read on people.

The High Priestess reversed

Reversed, you may be overriding your gut feeling, or something is being hidden. Step back from other people's opinions and check in with your own.

The Wispling take: Intuition · Mystery · Your inner voice

Between the dark pillar and the light one, a quiet ghost guards the secrets of the moon. She knows things. She's just not telling you yet. Sit still long enough and you might hear the answer yourself.

Journal prompt: When did a gut feeling turn out to be right?
