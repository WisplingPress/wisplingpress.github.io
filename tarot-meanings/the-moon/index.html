The Moon tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XVIII. The Moon lights the path only dimly. Things are not quite what they seem, so trust your intuition, pay attention to dreams and move carefully through uncertainty.

Upright keywords

intuitiondreamsuncertaintythe unknown

Reversed keywords

confusion liftingfacing fearsclarity returning
Yes or noMaybe: things are unclear
LoveMixed signals or hidden feelings. Trust your instincts and ask gently.
Work & moneyUnclear situations. Get the facts before making big decisions.

The Moon reversed

Reversed, confusion is clearing and fears are losing their grip. The truth is coming into focus.

The Wispling take: Dreams · Illusion · The unknown

The moon is sleepy, the path is winding and the frog in the pond clearly knows something you don't. Trust your dreams, but maybe check the map.
