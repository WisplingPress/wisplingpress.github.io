The Hanged Man tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XII. The Hanged Man hangs upside down and sees the world differently. It is a pause, a moment to let go of control and look at a situation from a new angle.

Upright keywords

pausesurrendernew perspectiveletting go

Reversed keywords

stallingresistanceneedless sacrifice
Yes or noNot yet
LoveSeeing a relationship from a new angle, or waiting for the right time.
Work & moneyA pause in plans. Use the time to rethink rather than force progress.

The Hanged Man reversed

Reversed, you may be stuck in limbo or resisting a pause you need. Ask what you are waiting for.

The Wispling take: Pause · Surrender · New perspective

Bats have always known this: hang upside down for a while and the whole world looks different. Sometimes the best move is no move at all.

Journal prompt: What would you notice if you flipped your point of view?
