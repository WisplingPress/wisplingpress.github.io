The Tower tarot card meaning

Major Arcana XVI. The Tower is sudden change: a shock, a revelation, something built on shaky ground coming down. It is dramatic, but it clears the way to rebuild on something truer.

Upright keywords

sudden changeupheavalrevelationawakening

Reversed keywords

avoiding disasterfear of changedelayed upheaval
Yes or noNo
LoveA revelation or a sudden shift that shows the truth of a relationship.
Work & moneyUnexpected change at work. Stay flexible and rebuild on solid foundations.

The Tower reversed

Reversed, you may be avoiding a change that needs to happen, or the upheaval is more internal than external.

The Wispling take: Sudden change · Revelation · Breakthrough

Lightning strikes, the crown flies off and the bats evacuate. Chaos? Definitely. But sometimes things need shaking loose so something better can be built.
