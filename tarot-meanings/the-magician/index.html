The Magician tarot card meaning

Major Arcana I. The Magician has every tool on the table and knows how to use them. This card says you already have what you need to turn an idea into something real, as long as you focus and act.

Upright keywords

willpowerskillfocusmaking it happen

Reversed keywords

scattered energyuntapped talenttrickery
Yes or noYes
LoveTaking the initiative, clear communication and showing up as yourself.
Work & moneyA strong card for launching, selling and learning new skills. Your abilities are your best asset right now.

The Magician reversed

Reversed, your energy may be scattered across too many things, or your skills are going unused. It can also warn of someone promising more than they can deliver.

The Wispling take: Willpower · Skill · Making it happen

A pumpkin, a candle, a cauldron and a bone: this cat has every tool it needs laid out on the table. The magic isn't really in the wand. It's in deciding to pick it up and use it.
